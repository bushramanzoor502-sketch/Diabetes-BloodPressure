Help centre

Support & quick guides

The things people ask about most: setup, troubleshooting and how to get your data out.

Log your first reading

Tap a card on the Home dashboard, or open the Tracking tab and pick a module, then tap Add Record. Set your numbers, choose a body state, adjust the date and time if needed, and save.

Set up reminders

Go to Settings → Notifications to manage reminder preferences, then set your daily water goal and schedule in the Water Reminder module. Allow the notification permission when prompted, or reminders cannot be delivered.

Steps not counting?

Grant the Physical activity permission, then exclude Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary from battery optimisation so Android does not stop the counter in the background. Some devices only update the sensor while the screen is on, so pull to refresh on the Step Counter screen to sync.

Export your data

Open Settings → Export Your Data, or use the export action on the Reports screen. Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary builds a CSV of every blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate and weight record and opens the share sheet so you can email or save it.

Fix or delete a record

Tap the record in Recent Records or All History to open it, use the menu at the top right to switch to edit mode, then update or delete it. Averages and charts recalculate straight away.

Moving to a new phone

Because data is stored only on your device, export a CSV before switching phones. There is no cloud account to sign back into. That is deliberate, and it is why the export button matters.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary work without internet?

Yes. Every feature, including logging, charts, reminders, the step counter and CSV export, runs entirely on your device. You can put the phone in airplane mode and nothing changes.

Do I need a smartwatch or a Bluetooth monitor?

No. Readings are entered by hand from whatever monitor you already own, and the step counter uses your phone's built in motion sensor.

Can I edit or delete a reading I entered wrong?

Yes. Open any record from history, switch it into edit mode, and update or delete it. All your averages and charts recalculate immediately.

How do I give my readings to my doctor?

Use Export Your Data in Settings, or the export action in Reports. Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary builds a CSV of every blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate and weight record and opens the Android share sheet so you can email, print or save it.

Why does the app ask for activity recognition?

That permission is what lets the phone's step sensor report your daily steps. If you decline it, every other module still works. You just won't see step counts.

Is this a medical device?

No. Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary is a personal wellness tracker for keeping your own records organised. It does not diagnose conditions or replace professional medical advice.

What happens to my data if I uninstall?

It's deleted with the app, because it was only ever stored locally. Export a CSV first if you want to keep your history.

My reminders stopped arriving

Check that notifications are still allowed for Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary in Android Settings, and that the app is excluded from battery optimisation. Aggressive battery savers on some devices will silently cancel scheduled alarms.

My step count reset or looks wrong

Step data comes from the phone's hardware sensor, which resets when the device restarts. Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary stores a daily summary so past days stay intact. If today's number looks low, open the Step Counter screen and refresh to resync with the sensor.

The BMI category doesn't match what I expected

BMI is calculated from the weight and height you entered on that record. Open the record in edit mode, correct the height (cm or ft/in) or weight (kg or lb), and the BMI and its category recalculate on save.

A chart is empty

Charts need at least one saved record in the selected period. Add a reading, or widen the range on the Reports screen. It defaults to Last 7 days and takes a custom range too.

I lost my data after reinstalling

Records are stored locally and are removed when the app is uninstalled or its storage is cleared. We have no server copy to restore from, so export a CSV regularly if you want a long term archive.
