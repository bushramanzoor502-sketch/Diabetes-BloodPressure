1. The short version

Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary is built to be a private notebook for your health readings. Everything you enter, from blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, weight, water intake and steps to notes and reminders, is written to a database on your own phone. We do not operate a server that receives your health data, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

No account. No cloud sync. No data sale. The only time your readings leave your device is when you personally use the export feature and choose an app to send the file to.

2. Information we handle

Health information you enter

  • Blood pressure readings (systolic, diastolic and an optional pulse), the body state you select, and notes
  • Blood sugar readings, the measurement type (fasting, before or after meal, random, bedtime), whether insulin was taken, body state and notes
  • Heart rate readings, body state and notes
  • Weight and height entries in the units you chose, the gender and age you enter for the BMI calculation, and the resulting BMI value and category
  • Water intake logged in millilitres against your daily goal
  • Dates, times and free text notes attached to any of the above

Device generated information

  • Daily step counts and the values derived from them (calories, distance, active time), read from your phone's hardware step sensor
  • An activity type signal, walking as opposed to cycling or driving, from Google Play services activity recognition. It keeps step counting accurate, is processed on your device, and is not sent to us

Preferences

  • Your onboarding status
  • Your daily step goal, and the step counter's own tracking state: the sensor baseline and last reading it uses to work out today's total
  • Your daily water goal, chosen cup size, water reminder interval, and whether water reminders are on
  • Your reminder schedules for blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate and weight
  • A master notifications switch

What we do not collect

  • No email address, phone number or password
  • No contacts, photos, files or precise location
  • No advertising identifier and no behavioural advertising profile

3. Where your data is stored

Your blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate, weight and BMI records and your daily step summaries are stored in a local database file inside the app's private storage area on your device. Your water intake logs, goals, reminder schedules and other preferences are stored in the app's private settings files. Both locations are sandboxed by Android, so other apps cannot read them.

Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary has no backend service for your health data. Nothing is uploaded, mirrored or backed up to servers we control, and cloud backup is switched off for this app. If you move to a new phone using Android's direct device to device transfer, Android itself copies your records across to that device; they never pass through a server of ours.

4. Permissions and why

  • Physical activity / activity recognition. Required by Android to read the step sensor data used by the step counter. Decline it and every other feature still works.
  • Notifications. Used to deliver your water reminders and the blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate and weight reading reminders you schedule, and to show the step counter's ongoing notification.
  • Foreground service (health). Keeps step counting accurate while the app is in the background.
  • Exact alarms. Makes reminders fire at the time you set instead of drifting.
  • Run at startup. Registers your reminders again after the phone restarts so they are not silently lost.
  • Internet. Used to download the illustration images shown with the Guides articles from Unsplash's image service, and to open external links such as the Play Store listing or this page. Those image requests tell Unsplash your IP address and rough region, under their own privacy policy. No health data is ever included in them, and your records are never transmitted.
  • Physical activity (Google Play services). The legacy form of the activity recognition permission, used on older Android versions for the same step tracking.
  • Foreground service. The base permission that sits alongside the health typed one above.

You can revoke any of these at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary → Permissions, and the app's own Settings → Permissions screen explains what each one is used for.

5. Sharing and export

The export feature builds a CSV file containing your blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate and weight or BMI records, and hands it to Android's standard share sheet. The weight section includes the gender and age you entered. Your step history and water intake logs are not included in the export. At that point you choose the destination: email, a messaging app, cloud storage or a printer. Once the file leaves Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary it is governed by the privacy policy of whichever app or service you selected, not by this one.

Exported files are written to the app's temporary cache and shared through a secure file provider.

6. Third parties

Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary does not embed advertising networks or analytics SDKs, and we never send your health records to any third party. Two outside components are involved, and neither one receives health data:

  • Unsplash. The illustration images in the Guides articles are downloaded from Unsplash's image service when you open an article. Like any image request on the web, this reveals your IP address and rough region to Unsplash, under their privacy policy.
  • Google Play services. Used on your device for activity recognition, so step counting can tell walking apart from cycling or driving. Links that open the Google Play Store are also handled by Google under their own terms.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Individual records: open any blood pressure, blood sugar, heart rate or weight entry from its history and delete it, and remove individual water entries from the Water Reminder screen.
  • Recorded daily step history cannot be deleted entry by entry inside the app. It goes when you clear the app's storage or uninstall.
  • Everything at once: uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android Settings, permanently removes the local database and all preferences.
  • Because we hold no copy, deletion on your device is complete deletion.

Keep a copy first. Export a CSV before uninstalling or clearing storage, because deleted local data cannot be recovered by us.

8. Children's privacy

Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. As all data stays on device and no account is created, no child's information is transmitted to us.

9. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export and erase your personal data. Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary satisfies these directly in the app: your records are visible and editable in full, the four reading types above are exportable as CSV, and records are erasable one by one, with clearing the app's storage removing everything at once. There is no request to file with us, because there is no copy of your data held by us.

10. Medical disclaimer

Blood Sugar & Diabetes Diary is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Reference ranges shown in the app and on this website are general adult guidance for convenience only. Readings are entered by you and are only as accurate as the monitor and method you used. Never delay seeking professional advice because of something you saw in this app, and contact emergency services immediately if you believe you are having a medical emergency.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date. Material changes affecting how data is handled will also be noted in the app's release notes.
